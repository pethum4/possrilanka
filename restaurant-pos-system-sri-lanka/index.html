Restaurant POS System Sri Lanka pain points

These are the workflow issues POS SL reviews before recommending software, hardware or package options.

  • Kitchen orders written by hand can be missed, delayed or prepared in the wrong order during peak service.
  • Waiters, cashiers and kitchen staff often need different access levels, but manual billing makes accountability weak.
  • Split bills, service charge, discounts, takeaway and delivery orders can slow the cashier if the workflow is not planned.
  • Ingredient stock and menu item sales are hard to compare when purchases, recipes and wastage are not connected.
  • Owners may not see waiter sales, table turnover, cancelled items or daily cash totals clearly.

Reports to plan

Good POS implementation should make owner reports easier to check after daily operations.

  • Daily restaurant sales
  • Waiter sales and cancellations
  • Table and order channel reports
  • Menu item movement
  • Cashier day-end totals
  • Stock and purchase reports

Restaurant POS System Sri Lanka workflow

Dine-in table workflow

Create orders by table, move or merge tables where required, print KOTs and settle bills with cashier control. This helps busy restaurants reduce handwritten order errors.

Takeaway and delivery workflow

Separate dine-in, takeaway and delivery orders so reports can show which channel is bringing sales and where preparation delays happen.

KOT and kitchen printer workflow

Send food items to kitchen printers and keep cashier receipts separate from preparation tickets. Printer routing should be planned by counter, kitchen distance and menu sections.

Menu, modifiers and permissions

Plan menu categories, add-ons, service charge, taxes if applicable, waiter permissions, cancelled item control and manager approval for sensitive actions.

Recipe and ingredient stock direction

Where recipe tracking is required, connect menu sales with ingredient movement so purchasing and wastage can be reviewed more accurately.

Hardware recommendations

  • Touch POS terminal or billing PC for the cashier counter
  • Thermal receipt printer for customer bills
  • LAN kitchen printer for KOT workflow where the kitchen is separate
  • Cash drawer for cashier control
  • Barcode scanner only if packaged items or barcode stock are used

Implementation timeline

  1. Review the counter workflow, branch count, item types and staff roles.
  2. Prepare item categories, opening stock, suppliers, users and receipt details.
  3. Configure POS SL software with printer, scanner and cash drawer where required.
  4. Train cashiers, managers and owners on daily billing, returns, stock and reports.
  5. Run a controlled go-live with support for first-day issues and report checks.

FAQs

Does POS SL support restaurant KOT printing?

Yes. KOT printer workflow can be planned with compatible kitchen printers and menu routing.

Can the system handle dine-in, takeaway and delivery?

Yes. These order types can be separated so the cashier workflow and reports are clearer.

Can POS SL work offline during busy hours?

Offline or offline plus cloud setup can be discussed when uninterrupted billing is important.

Can waiters have limited permissions?

Yes. Staff access can be planned so sensitive actions such as discounts, cancellations and reports are controlled.

Can ingredient stock be tracked?

Ingredient or recipe stock can be reviewed as part of the setup requirement because the workflow varies by restaurant.
