POS System Pricing Sri Lanka guide for Sri Lankan buyers

POS system price in Sri Lanka depends on software package, hardware, installation, data preparation, training, support and customization. POS SL publishes package signals while keeping hardware and custom work quotation-based.

Software cost

POS SL currently lists cloud, offline and offline plus cloud packages. The right package depends on whether the business needs remote access, local-only billing or both.

Hardware cost

Hardware cost depends on devices such as POS machines, receipt printers, barcode scanners, cash drawers, kitchen printers and label printers. Hardware pricing should be confirmed through Pixellark.

Installation and training

Implementation may include item setup, printer configuration, staff training, report review and go-live support. Complex data cleanup or multi-branch planning can affect effort.

Customization cost

Custom reports, special workflows and integration requests should be quoted separately after reviewing the requirement.
