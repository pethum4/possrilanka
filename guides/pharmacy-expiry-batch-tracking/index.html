Pharmacy Expiry and Batch Tracking

How Sri Lankan pharmacies should plan medicine batch, expiry, GRN and reorder workflows.

Clear answer

Pharmacy expiry and batch tracking helps record medicine stock by purchase batch and expiry date so the pharmacy can review short-expiry items, supplier purchases, returns and reorder needs more clearly.

Sri Lankan POS context

Why expiry tracking matters

Expired medicine can create financial loss and serious operational risk. Expiry reports help staff review short-expiry stock before it becomes unsellable.

Batch workflow

Batch numbers connect stock to supplier purchases. This is useful when different batches of the same medicine have different expiry dates or purchase costs.

GRN and supplier workflow

A good pharmacy POS should support supplier purchases, GRNs, returns, damaged stock and purchase cost changes.

Staff permissions

Returns, purchase changes and expiry-sensitive adjustments should be controlled by role so records stay reliable.

FAQs

Does every pharmacy item need batch tracking?

The workflow depends on the pharmacy. Batch and expiry should be planned for items where control is required.

Can POS SL show expiry reports?

Expiry reporting can be planned when item data and batch/expiry workflow are maintained.

Can pharmacy staff search by generic name?

The item structure can include generic and brand names for easier search.
