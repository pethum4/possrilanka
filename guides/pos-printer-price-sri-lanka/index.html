Clear answer

POS printer price in Sri Lanka depends on printer type, connection method, brand, paper size, warranty and availability. Choose the printer after confirming whether you need customer receipts, kitchen KOT printing, barcode labels or network printing.

Practical checklist

  • Receipt or label use
  • USB/LAN/Bluetooth
  • Paper width
  • Kitchen distance
  • Print volume
  • Warranty
  • POS compatibility

Sri Lankan POS context

Thermal receipt printers

Thermal printers are common for retail, pharmacy, grocery and supermarket counters. Check paper width, print speed, durability and USB/LAN/Bluetooth support.

Kitchen printers

Restaurants and cafes may need kitchen printers for KOT. LAN printers are often useful when kitchen areas are away from the cashier counter.

Barcode label printers

Textile, fashion, retail and grocery businesses may need label printers when products do not already have usable barcodes.

Total printer cost

Consider printer price, paper or label supply, warranty, cable/network setup and POS software compatibility.

FAQs

Is USB or LAN better for POS printers?

USB is simple for one counter. LAN is useful for kitchen or shared network printing.

Do all shops need label printers?

No. Label printers are needed only when the business must print barcodes for items.

Can POS SL check printer compatibility?

Yes. Printer model and connection type should be reviewed before setup.
