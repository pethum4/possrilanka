Understanding POS Billing System Price in Sri Lanka

When investing in a POS billing system for a Sri Lankan retail shop, supermarket, pharmacy, or restaurant, pricing is divided into two core pillars: Hardware Equipment and POS Software Licensing. Many vendors quote ambiguous lump sums with hidden monthly maintenance or unverified software fees. At POS Sri Lanka (Pixellark Solution), we practice 100% price transparency.

🖥️

1. POS Machine Hardware

Hardware includes the counter billing terminal (capacitive touch screen all-in-one, dual-screen display, mobile Android terminal, or desktop PC), heavy-duty power supplies, and alloy stands. Quality commercial hardware ranges from Rs. 48,000 to Rs. 145,000 depending on display size, dual-screen requirements, and processor speeds.

💾

2. Genuine POS Software

Software handles your barcodes, receipts, stock tracking, and profit analytics. Choose between a One-time Offline License (Rs. 17,500) with zero recurring fees, or System-Navigator Cloud Sync ($3 USD/month) for live mobile phone access from anywhere in the world.

🖨️

3. Counter Add-on Accessories

Peripherals tailor the system to your counter volume: 80mm thermal receipt printers (Rs. 24,500), 1D/2D QR barcode scanners (Rs. 12,500), automatic kick cash drawers (Rs. 16,500), and barcode sticker printers (Rs. 38,500).

Frequently Asked Questions on POS System Prices in Sri Lanka

Essential answers on hardware warranties, delivery options, software licensing, and payment methods.

What is the average POS billing system price in Sri Lanka? ▾

In Sri Lanka, a complete POS billing system typically ranges from Rs. 45,000 for handheld Android billing terminals up to Rs. 180,000 - Rs. 260,000 for heavy-duty commercial dual-screen all-in-one touch workstations. Standalone POS billing software starts from Rs. 17,500 for a lifetime offline license, while cloud sync packages start from Rs. 25,500 setup.

Does the package price include both POS hardware and software? ▾

Yes! POS Sri Lanka offers complete ready-to-bill packages that bundle commercial grade POS machine hardware (touch terminal or PC) along with genuine POS SL or System-Navigator software, receipt printers, barcode scanners, and cash drawers. You can configure and calculate the exact combination on this page.

What is the difference between offline POS and cloud sync POS in Sri Lanka? ▾

POS SL Offline Software (Rs. 17,500 one-time) runs 100% locally on your computer with zero monthly server fees and never stops working during internet drops. System-Navigator Active Cloud Sync POS (Rs. 67,500 + $3 USD/mo) gives you the best of both worlds: fast offline billing at the counter and automatic cloud synchronization for remote phone reporting and multi-branch management.

Can I choose my own hardware add-ons like thermal printers and barcode scanners? ▾

Yes. You can customize your POS package with 80mm thermal receipt printers (Rs. 24,500), high-speed 1D/2D barcode scanners (Rs. 12,500), heavy-duty cash drawers (Rs. 16,500), barcode label printers (Rs. 38,500), and kitchen KOT printers (Rs. 29,500).
